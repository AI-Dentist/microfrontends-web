Visible 3px focus / 3px offset uses S geometry and proposed T colors; dark-primary focus on white is 7.56:1. No motion is used; reduced-motion rules are explicit.

Structural and contrast checks are not a browser render or runtime test. This sync does not claim production readiness, unified dark support, or a working ERP.
